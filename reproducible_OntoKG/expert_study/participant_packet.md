# OntoKG-EQ Expert Study — Participant Packet

*Estimated time: 20–30 minutes. There are no right or wrong answers; we are evaluating the explanations, not you.*

## Consent

You are invited to take part in a short study on how financial analysts read automatically generated analytical statements. Participation is voluntary and anonymous; you may stop at any time. We record only your ratings and role/experience — no personal identifiers. Your responses will be reported only in aggregate.

## Instructions

For each item you will see a short **analyst statement (Version A)**. Rate how much you trust it and how complete its justification is, and note what you would need to verify it. **Then** the facilitator will reveal the **same statement with its supporting evidence (Version B)**; rate it again and say which version you would rather receive.

Rating scale (circle one): **1 = not at all … 4 = moderately … 7 = completely.**

> Do not look at Version B before you have rated Version A.
