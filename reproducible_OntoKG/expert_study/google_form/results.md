# Expert study — results (from Google Form)

Respondents: 17; items: 8; paired trust responses: 136.

- **Trust:** A mean 2.93 (median 2.0) vs B mean 5.79 (median 6.0); Wilcoxon signed-rank p = 4.081e-22 (n=128 non-tied pairs), rank-biserial r = 0.97
- **Completeness of justification:** A mean 2.10 (median 1.5) vs B mean 5.44 (median 5.0); Wilcoxon signed-rank p = 1.272e-22 (n=128 non-tied pairs), rank-biserial r = 0.99
- **Preference:** B 134, A 0, no-difference 2; among A/B choices prefer-B 134/134 = 100% (binomial p = 1.818e-12).

## Ready-to-paste Section 7.6 paragraph

> We ran the pre-registered within-subject study with 17 participants [roles/experience], each rating 8 OntoKG-EQ statements first as result-only notes (Version A) and then with the provenance-grounded evidence bundle (Version B). Adding the evidence raised mean trust from 2.93 to 5.79 on a 7-point scale (Wilcoxon signed-rank p = 4.081e-22) and mean perceived completeness from 2.10 to 5.44; 100% of participants preferred the evidence-grounded version (binomial p = 1.818e-12). This converts the paper's structural faithfulness guarantee into a measured improvement in analyst trust and verifiability.
