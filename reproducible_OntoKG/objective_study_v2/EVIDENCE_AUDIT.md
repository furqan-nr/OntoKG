# Objective human-results availability audit — 2026-10-16

Status: **NO OBJECTIVE ONTOKG HUMAN RESPONSES FOUND; NEW STUDY NOT RUN.**

The two retained private original-study workbooks were inspected for dimensions and column headers only. Each contains 17 respondent rows and 52 columns: timestamp, consent, role, experience, then eight sets of trust, perceived completeness, requested verification information and preference questions. No participant answers, identities, exact timestamps or free text were exported in this audit.

Neither contains correctness-scored claim judgments, source-cell retrieval answers, randomized display assignment or observed per-task durations. A submission timestamp is not task duration. Requested verification information is not observed verification success. Neither workbook permits retrospective estimation of objective audit improvement. Duplicate workbook filenames are not independent samples.

The new response template remains header-only. Prepared packets and automated replay are not human sessions. To obtain objective results, authors must conduct real consented sessions under the applicable institutional approval/exemption, freeze the instrument and analysis plan, and provide a privacy-approved anonymous nine-field response export, allocation procedure, and attrition/deviation summary. A single completed person tests feasibility only; general improvement claims need a prospective precision/power plan.

Safeguards added: reject nonfinite/invalid times, malformed fields and unknown answer categories; refuse preparation when the template contains records; mark the old item-level Google Form report as superseded. Unit-test fixtures are software checks only and never saved as participant records.
