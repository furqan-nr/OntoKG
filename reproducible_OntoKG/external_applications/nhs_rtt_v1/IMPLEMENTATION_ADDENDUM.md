# Implementation addendum — 18 October 2026

The frozen `PROTOCOL.md` is preserved unchanged. Its optional arithmetic-extension
warning anticipated that multiplication might require a new interpreter version.
Implementation instead compiles the fixed nonnegative integer factors 100 and
92 into balanced repeated addition in the existing `boundary-1` expression
language. The checker source remains unchanged; this is not zero engineering
effort or unchanged reuse of the whole financial pipeline.

New engineering comprises an independent source adapter, a two-role application
vocabulary/contract, the bounded fixed-factor compiler, source selectors, an
independent source oracle and separately authored SHACL-SPARQL/availability
baseline. Eighteen weekly bands are aggregated in the source adapter upstream
of the two-role checker. This is not a claim of native eighteen-role aggregation
or a new general arithmetic semantics.

## Executed scope and interpretation

workbook on eighteen weekly counts, total and within-18-week numerator; stored
ratios use the fixed `1e-12` tolerance in ratio units. Exact cross-products give
91 at/above the inclusive 92% threshold and 441 below. These are pathways, not
unique people, clinical outcomes, legal-compliance judgments or quality rankings.

The publisher workbook roster also contains D1V5N, ACES BOLTON, with zero total
but no selected CSV row. Its application inputs remain unavailable, not fabricated
zero counts or a target failure. This yields 533 frames, not 533 eligible rates.

selected CSV rows does not establish a missing submission or publisher defect.

Generated explanation views are this application's outputs, not NHS-provided
explanations. Their recorded source selectors reference 242,934 source cells;
these are not independent observations or authenticated patient-level lineage.
CSV and workbook share the reporting collection. No natural reconciliation
errors were found, and no production deployment, practitioner adoption or human
audit-performance benefit has been demonstrated.

The independent matched baseline directly reconstructs RDF pools and uses
actual SHACL validation plus explicit availability/readiness checks. It caches
identical pools within each frame and configures Decimal precision sufficiently
for exact integer cross-products. This is transparent comparator engineering,
not evidence of weaker SHACL expressiveness. Three matching pool outcomes are
not three independent samples. Consult `application/results.json` for executed
counts, hashes, software versions and descriptive timings.
